# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: investors and funding partners evaluating Geoxs Biotech as an early-stage biotech venture — assessing credibility, IP, technical viability, and growth path before committing capital or a partnership.

Secondary: industrial and research partners the site explicitly invites ("whether you work in waste management, materials, or research") — waste-management companies, materials manufacturers, and research institutions considering licensing, supply, or collaboration.

## Product Purpose

Geoxs Biotech Private Limited develops enzyme- and microbe-based plastic degradation technology that breaks down plastic waste into reusable raw material, alongside a complementary algae-derived bioplastic materials platform. The site exists to convert visitor trust into partnership: funding, licensing deals, supply partnerships, and research collaboration. Success is a visitor believing the venture is scientifically credible and far enough along to be worth engaging, then acting on the "Partner With Us" CTA.

## Positioning

Geoxs combines two complementary mechanisms other single-focus ventures don't pair: enzyme/microbial degradation that turns existing plastic pollution back into raw material, and an algae-derived bioplastic platform that prevents new pollution from the outset. The venture holds active IP with a further patent underway covering its prototype degradation system, and was a NewGen IEDC selected venture (India's national innovation and entrepreneurship program) — third-party validation that anchors its credibility claims.

## Operating Context

- Incorporated 13 September 2022, Tamil Nadu, India. Legal entity: Geoxs Biotech Private Limited (CIN: U37100TZ2022PTC040037).
- Pre-revenue, staged roadmap: lab validation → pilot production → commercial production → international deployment (a planned India–UK structure alongside named UK partner EcoAlgaForm).
- The site is a static, no-backend deployment (GitHub Pages, canonical `athul-seban.github.io/geoxs-biotech/`). The contact form has no server: submitting it opens the visitor's email client via a `mailto:` link rather than posting anywhere.
- Founder Hiba Habeebulla (BSc Microbiology, MSc Biomedical & Molecular Science) and Co-founder Prabakaran Vinothkumar are the named team; Hiba's LinkedIn is linked from her card.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No backend, CMS, or form-processing service exists or is planned for this surface — the mailto-based contact flow is the real mechanism, not a placeholder to replace.
- Revenue model spans four named paths: technology licensing, enzyme/product sales, bioplastic materials sales, and strategic partnerships (including EcoAlgaForm).
- No pricing, customer logos, case studies, testimonials, or press coverage exist. None should be fabricated or implied by future work.

## Brand Commitments

- Company name and legal facts (CIN, incorporation date, jurisdiction) are fixed and must stay accurate.
- The logo — a green-to-teal molecular chain mark paired with a PT Serif wordmark ("GEOXS BIOTECH" / "PRIVATE LIMITED.") — is a fixed, recognizable brand asset per the user's explicit direction: redesigns should keep the mark and wordmark as-is and confine visual reinvention to the surrounding UI (surfaces, materials, motion, and body/UI typography).

## Evidence on Hand

- Founder and co-founder bios and one LinkedIn profile (Hiba Habeebulla) are real and on the site.
- `NEWGEN IEDC SELECTED VENTURE` credibility claim and incorporation facts are real, sourced from the existing site copy.
- `Geoxs_Biotech_Summary.pdf` exists at the project root as a supporting investor document but is not currently linked from the site.
- Absent: pricing, testimonials, case studies, customer/partner logos, press mentions. Do not invent these.

## Product Principles

1. Scientific credibility over decoration — every visual or motion choice should read as backing real IP and prototype work, not manufacturing hype the venture hasn't earned yet.
2. Investor and partner trust is earned through clarity and evidence, not persuasion tricks — no invented proof, urgency, or social validation.
3. The registered brand identity (name, legal facts, logo mark, and wordmark) is fixed ground truth that survives even a full visual reinvention of the surrounding design.
4. The existing narrative arc — problem → technology → viability → roadmap → team → contact — is the working structure; preserve it across visual redesigns unless the user explicitly asks to restructure content.
